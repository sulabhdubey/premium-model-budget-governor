# Study Notes (Authored Fixture)

## A: Broad baseline
Four unrelated reviews used a total of 16.0 projected credits. All four passed
their task-specific criteria. Input scope and host cache state were not recorded.

## B: Early candidate report and correction
A compact Astra workflow initially reported 7.2 projected credits across four
different reviews. Two preparation calls and a failed retry were omitted. Their
receipts add 5.6, making the corrected total 12.8. Three of four final answers
passed. These were not the same reviews as A.

## C: Matched follow-up
One identical review, source snapshot and rubric was completed successfully by
both arms. Direct Astra used 3.2 projected credits and prepared Astra used 3.8
including preparation. Direct ran first; cache conditions were not controlled.
All recorded usage was token-derived, not provider-billed credits.

## D: Account observation
The account's remaining weekly allowance fell from 60% to 45% during the test
window. Two unrelated chats were also active. No per-task weekly debit was exposed.
