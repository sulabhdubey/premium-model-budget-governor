# Product Brief (Authored Fixture)

Premium Model Budget Governor is an independent local tool for deliberate use
of Astra within a task budget. Astra Preferred preserves premium participation;
Economy is an explicit user choice. It is not an OpenAI product.

The workbench previews estimates, asks for approval, runs read-only filesystem
tasks, and displays host-reported token counts with projected credits. It does
not control other existing chats, impose provider billing caps, or know exact
per-task weekly allowance charges. Inherited connector permissions still apply.

Earlier small experiments found additional handoffs could cost more than direct
Astra. Broad savings and unaided nontechnical usability are not established.
We need reports on setup friction, useful answers and misunderstood estimates.

Sulabh Dubey originated the idea and directs the product and research. Codex
performed engineering under his direction. Apache-2.0 licensed. No customer,
endorsement, download or star counts have been provided.
