# Origin and Credits

## Why This Exists

Premium Model Budget Governor began with a concrete operating problem experienced
by Sulabh Dubey while using Codex with GPT-6 Astra. One weekly allowance was
consumed in roughly one day. After using a reset, the replacement allowance was
again down to 16% by the next day and later reached 11% while the project was
being prepared for launch.

Those readings are the creator's observed account-capacity states. They are not
a controlled provider benchmark and should not be interpreted as universal token
or billing measurements.

The experience exposed a more useful question than simply avoiding premium
models:

> How can we use as much of Astra's capability as possible while keeping
> workflow burn closer to Sol?

The answer became a hybrid operating model. Efficient models perform broad
exploration and implementation. Premium models receive compact, scanned,
high-leverage decisions. Prompt-free telemetry records whether the premium turn
actually helped.
